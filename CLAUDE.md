# Working rules for this project

Animal Walk: a game in Godot 4.8 (GL Compatibility) for PC and phones (Android first).
The idea, the hero and the rules of the game are still to be described by the player: ask, do not invent.
This file holds the rules and the player's decisions with their reasons. Update it in the SAME commit as a change of behaviour.

## Working with the player
- The player writes Russian; answer in Russian. Be short and concrete. Code, comments and docs may be in Russian or English (keep one per file).
- The player is the designer. **Ask before any design decision the player did not state** (a look, a rule of the game, a number that changes how it plays). A question with two or three concrete options is fine. Do not "improve" what was described.
- Do exactly what is asked, nothing next to it. A forwarded wish list is not a task: discuss and propose first.
- Before changing an existing mechanic, find out why it is the way it is (here, in docs/, in the code); if it is not written, ask.
- Report honestly: what was measured, what only the phone can confirm, what was not tested.
- Show the result: a screenshot or a short video of every visible change.

## The player's decisions so far
- Inspired by DOGWALK (Blender Studio): keep its look and juice, but our own game. Their CC BY assets are NOT used; we build our own paper-craft look (option "A").
- The leash pair: **the bunny (hero, Chobby Bun) holds the leash, TOFU (red panda) is on it (TOFU wears a red collar with a brass ring; the leash clips to the ring. The ring slides round the collar toward the leash (the player asked: the rope went through TOFU's body when the bunny was behind), and the rope lies over TOFU's torso and tail and over the bunny's torso and hips (capsules, "capsules" in each `Actor` map; the bunny's hip one is thin so her leash hand stays outside it) instead of passing through them. It sits tilted, higher at the back, above the shirt's neckline and clear of the chin; a positive "tilt" lowers the front. The colours are my choice, not yet confirmed), and TOFU drags the bunny along** (like DOGWALK, where the dog pulls the kid).
- Control (the player's rule): while the bunny holds the leash, the player steers TOFU. The action button makes the bunny hammer a stake in and tie the leash to it: now the player steers the bunny. Next to the stake (1.4 m) the button pulls it out and control goes back to TOFU.
  - Co-op is planned (the player): each walks by itself and only the leash ties them. Pulling apart they pull each other (the overshoot is taken back half from each; a quicker one wins and drags the other); one without a player (standing) is led by the other: TOFU led by the bunny follows her, the bunny led by TOFU can't keep up (her dragged look). Their bodies bump instead of passing through (radii 0.3 + 0.25 m; the one walking into the other is stopped, a standing one is not pushed). `LeashRules.tug` / `separate`, `Main._move`; `partner_input` is the other one's wish for scripts until co-op exists; `tests/test_coop.gd`.
  - The switch button (the player asked, to test each character; top right under the eye, Tab on a PC) gives the player the other one at any time, the stake in or not; the stake and its pick-up still hand the player the bunny / TOFU as before. Tied to the stake, TOFU steered by the player goes round it within the leash.
  - TOFU's right paw on a second stick (the player asked; bottom-right corner, the stake button above it; I J K L on a PC; shown while the player steers TOFU): the stick points the arm round the shoulder (left / right up to 69 degrees out and 40 across the chest, up / down 69 / 63 degrees) and the paw reaches out along that line until it meets something: the ground, a rock, bush or trunk, or the bunny's body ("touch" capsules in her map, radii from her skin); a line blocked closer than 0.2 m to the shoulder is lowered step by step instead of folding the arm up (or the arm stays down). Let go and the arm comes back down (0.4 s). The forearm and paw reach as one segment (IK), the wrist follows a critically damped spring solved exactly (an explicit step blew up on long frames). `PawReach`, `Gait._paw_reach`, `ArmStick`; `tests/test_paw.gd`. My numbers.
  - Her butt, thighs and belly are soft and springy too (the player: they are big; like her chest): `SoftFlesh` gives each vertex of her skin and shorts (not her top: its hem turned inside out) a share in three regions (left butt-and-thigh, right, belly; from the butt, thigh and lower-spine bone weights) kept in the mesh (CUSTOM0); a spring per region riding on the pelvis / lower spine (`Gait._flesh_springs`: 5 Hz, damping 0.35, at most 1.8 cm; at 3.2-4 Hz a run's steps hit its limit every step) moves them in the soft-press shader. A paw pressing them moves the region away (60% of the press; the rest is the skin's dent) and it follows the paw back. `tests/test_flesh.gd`. My numbers.
  - The springs (chest, flesh) had a fault: the anchor was taken at the end of each substep, so the mass ran a substep ahead of the body (1.2 cm walking, 2.8 running: what pinned the chest forward at its limit when running). Fixed; they also feel the body's motion eased between physics ticks (`Gait.motion_fix`: with more frames than ticks, a 90-120 Hz screen, it moved in steps that hit them like blows).
  - Easter egg (the player's): her chest is soft to the paw: it may sink 3 cm into it, her skin is pushed out of the paw's ball by the soft-press shader (the same giving as lying on the ground, "paw" uniform), and the chest's tip is pushed by the paw and swings back after (`Gait.soft_poke`). Realistic, not exaggerated: about 1-2 cm of push.
  - My defaults, not yet confirmed by the player: TOFU just stands and watches the bunny while tied; run = stick pushed far or Shift; leash 3 m; heights bunny 1.6 m, TOFU 1.1 m.
  - Camera (the player: closer, third person, turn and zoom like mobile shooters): `CameraRig` orbits the character you steer (leaning 15% toward the other one). Phone: drag on the right part of the screen turns it (round and up / down, 5-75 degrees above the horizon), a two-finger pinch zooms (2-10 m); the left 60% is the walking stick, the action button keeps its touches. PC: right mouse drag turns (or the left one on the right part: mouse emulates touch), the wheel zooms. Walking is relative to the camera (the stick's up walks away from it). Never under the ground (0.35 m above it). Start: 4.5 m, 24 degrees, FOV 50. My numbers; `tests/test_camera.gd`.
  - First / third person (the player asked for a switch: an eye button top right, V on a PC): first person puts the camera at the steered character's eyes ("eye" in its map: metres up and forward of the head bone, measured on the face; TOFU's set back from its long snout so the collar shows below), hides its head (shrunk and put away inside the chest, so neither the head nor the neck's skin gets in the way; the eyes are taken before), lets the view go 83 degrees down and 52 up while the head follows only part of it (at most 34 degrees down: the eyes do the rest, no broken neck), and turns the standing body to the view. The view is the body's own (the player saw her turn round 180 degrees while the view stayed): it starts where the character faces; while the stick is held (or the bunny hammers) the body turns by itself and the view turns with it, the stick keeps the direction it was pushed in (stick back: she turns round and walks off, the view with her) and a drag steers (`CameraRig.body_leads`). Looking down the bunny sees her chest, TOFU its paws and the leash; a little of TOFU's chin (skinned to the neck, which carries the collar) can show at the bottom. `tests/test_first_person.gd`. The buttons' positions come from the screen's size (their own stayed 0, which had put the stake button off the screen).
- Genre (the player): a walking / exploration simulation.
- Setting: a big beach island to explore (about 220 m across; the player asked for big): bays and headlands, a sand beach all round rising from the sea at a steady slope, wet sand at the waterline, grassy hills with flowering bushes inside, sandy hollows with palm groves between them, rocks at the water's edge and as outcrops on the hills, dune grass, shells and starfish by the water. Turquoise sea with a thin foam line exactly at the waterline (signed depth in the water mesh's UV). Walkers may wade in up to their ankles (0.12 m, about 2.7 m out); a paw in the sea throws a splash and leaves no print, and the leash floats on the water. The start is on the north beach facing the sea. Vegetation is cut into 32 m pieces drawn only within 60 m of the camera; obstacles sit in a 4 m grid.
- Animations: real clips, not sine waves (the player found the code-made walk unnatural). Walking, jogging, sprinting and standing come from Quaternius' Universal Animation Library (CC0, `assets/anim/ual_standard.glb`), read by `scripts/clip_source.gd` and put on our skeletons at run time by `scripts/retarget.gd`: each mapped bone takes the clip's rotation away from the clip's rest, applied over our rest after lining our rest up with the clip's (so Chobby's V-sign rest does not matter); the hips' bob comes back as a body offset. Clips blend idle / walk / jog / sprint by speed and are timed by the distance walked (step length from the clip's own foot sweep, scaled between hip height and leg length), so planted feet barely slide (about 20%, tested). `Gait` (`scripts/gait.gd`) adds on top: head looks (at the partner, or around when idle), ears and tail on springs, breathing, the leash hand, three hammer strikes for the stake, tiredness. The old sine-wave walk is kept only as a fallback for a model without "clips".
  - Running, the bunny swayed oddly (the player). Two causes, both fixed (`tests/test_sway.gd`): her rest's chest is turned 21 degrees and its spine bent sideways while the shoulders are level, so the chest, upper spine and neck are lined up with the clip only by turning the shoulder line about the vertical (`Retarget.SIDE_PAIR`, `_yaw_align`; their lean stays as modelled: lining the chest bone, which leans 30 degrees back in her rest, up with the clip's tipped her chest forward ~45 degrees and the player saw it hang; breasts now point 45 degrees below level standing, 38 before the clips, 74 when it hung) (not the pelvis, which tilts with its hips in her one-leg rest); and the library's jog throws the torso about like a cartoon (the chest rolls 25 degrees each way, twists 35), so she keeps half of that swing around the clip's average ("torso_swing" 0.5; my number). TOFU is unchanged. The palm is turned toward the thigh every frame after the arm swings.
  - She walks like a woman (the player: the clips' walk looked odd on her; there is no free woman's walk clip, so it is layered on in `Gait._feminine_walk`, "feminine" 1.0 in her map): the hips sway over the standing leg (2.8 cm), the free side of the pelvis drops (0.10 rad) and swings forward with the stepping leg (0.12 rad), steps narrow toward one line (14 cm apart instead of 21), chest carried up. The thighs are re-aimed so each foot stays where the clip put it (planted feet still hold). 40% of it in a run. My numbers; `tests/test_feminine.gd`.
  - Rabbit legs (the player chose them over straight human ones): in her T-pose she stands on the front of her paws, heel up 25 degrees, the leg folded like a Z with the knee 12 cm (tool metres; ~8 cm in game) in front of the hip-ankle line, hips 2 cm lower; both legs fold alike (knees 8 / 8 cm in game). The retarget keeps this leg shape ("clip_keep": the clips' leg motion goes on top of it instead of lining the legs up with the clip's straight ones). "sole" 0.032 lifts her so the paw skin stands on the ground. `tests/test_rabbit_legs.gd`. My numbers.
  - Army crawl (the player chose it, "по-пластунски"; no free clip has one): `tools/make_crawl.gd` poses the clip library's own skeleton (limbs by ground targets, two-bone IK) into Crawl_Fwd (on the belly, the upper body on the forearms so the chest is clear, one forearm reaching while the opposite knee draws up to the side, 1.7 s, 0.64 m per cycle at the clip's size: each forearm pulls 0.32 m) and Crawl_Idle, saved as `assets/anim/crawl.res`, which ClipSource loads with the library. `gait.crawling` (set by the game) eases her down in 0.6 s; the retarget blends standing to crawl; while crawling: no feminine sway, pull lean, tiredness or leash grip (both arms pull), palms down, fingers flat, no footprints, TOFU's tail lies back along the ground. Her hips (the player asked to study them): the pelvis rolls 0.22 rad toward the drawn-up knee's side, that hip up and the weight on the other (was 0.07 and a quarter cycle late), the ribcage turns 60% of it back so the shoulders stay level, and the drawn-up thigh lies low on its inner side. Thick bodies would sink, so each character is raised while lying ("crawl_lift": TOFU 9 cm; the bunny only 3 cm: the player wants her chest to rest on the ground) and any hand, elbow, foot or knee that would still sink is bent onto the ground ("crawl_thick": TOFU's paws and sleeves are 1.9 times as thick). Her skin gives against the ground per vertex (the player: soft, elastic): `SoftPress` swaps her (unshaded, textured) materials for `shaders/soft_press.gdshader`, which, while she lies, squeezes skin that would go under the ground up onto it (the deeper the more, within 5 cm) and spreads it a little sideways; her chest's spring may reach the ground. A foot with no toe bone (TOFU) is lined up as pointing forward along the ground (lined up as the shin, its shoe turned its toes up: the player saw TOFU broken). `tests/test_crawl.gd`: nothing sinks more than 2.5 cm. My numbers.
  - Low passages (the player: walkers get down by themselves under something low): `LowPassages` lays 12 fallen palm trunks across two rocks around the island (one 8 m from the start, across the beach), the trunk 0.55-0.7 m over the ground. Under a trunk a walker crawls, sized by its height (a cycle moves the bunny 0.59 m, TOFU 0.40 m) and never quicker than a cycle in 1.4 s: at most 0.42 m/s for the bunny, 0.29 for TOFU (at 0.7 m/s her crawl ran a cycle in 0.5 s and the player found it unnaturally fast; `Retarget.crawl_speed`), the rocks block, the leash lies over the trunk, the camera looks lower at a crawler. Other plants and rocks keep clear of them. `tests/test_passages.gd`. My numbers.
  - The bunny's arms are NOT copied from the clips (copied rotations wrung her wrists and threw the elbows out: her rest is far from the clip's). Gait starts them from "arms_down", turns the forearm (over its twist bones) so the palm faces the thigh with the wrist straight, then aims upper arm and forearm by the shortest turn along the clip's own arm directions (`Retarget.arm_dirs`), so the swing comes from the clip and the twist stays natural (`tests/test_arms.gd`). The player asked for normal arms.
  - The bunny's right hand holds the leash by two-bone IK and follows it strongly (the player asked), but within what a shoulder can do: at most 30 degrees across the chest, 115 degrees back along her side (a leash behind her is reached for along the right side), 65 degrees above the horizon; the elbow bends only the natural way. Slack leash: the hand stays partly toward the leash with a soft elbow and rides the steps; taut leash: the arm straightens toward TOFU. Her right fingers close around the leash while she holds it. The arm moves slowly and smoothly on purpose (eased in over about half a second; the wrist goal follows a soft 1.1 Hz spring, so it never snaps when TOFU turns).
  - Pulled along she can't keep up (the player asked; it replaced my lean back against the leash): while the leash is taut the whole body leans forward after it (0.18 rad), the spine bends forward (0.22), the chest turns so the leash shoulder leads (0.30), the free arm swings back for balance (0.45), the head keeps her eyes on TOFU; it grows with the leash's pull (`Gait._pulled_along`, `tests/test_pulled.gd`). My numbers.
  - The bunny can't keep up (the player's request): dragged at a run she tires over about 4 s ("tires" in her map; TOFU never tires). Worn out: shorter frantic steps, head droops, the free left arm swings out for balance (smoothly in time: driven by a fraction of the step phase it jumped 18 cm in a frame at every step and the player saw it jerk; now about 25 degrees out), and every 1.2-2.8 s a stumble (a knee buckles, the body pitches forward, arms thrown out). Stopped while more than half tired, she bends over at the hips, a hand toward the knee, panting, until mostly rested (about 4.5 s to recover fully). Looks only: it does not change speeds or the rules. My numbers, not yet confirmed by the player.
  - A gap opened between Chobby's top and her skin when the chest moved (the player asked to fix it): the model's top hangs mostly on the spine while the skin under it follows the chest bones. `SkinFit` (`scripts/skin_fit.gd`, "cloth" in `Actor.BUNNY`) gives the top and the shorts the bone weights of the nearest skin (three nearest, blended; arm and hand skin skipped, or the armhole would follow the arm), lifted 5 mm off the skin so it never shows through. About 0.17 s at load on a PC (`tests/test_cloth.gd`: 95% of the top within 6 mm of the skin while running; was 20 mm).
  - When the leash end is re-tied (stake in or out), the rope is laid out again at rest (`Leash.reattach_b`), so it does not whip.
  - The leash is drawn as a smooth curve near its simulated points (corners cut three times, `Leash.smooth`): slack rope piled at TOFU's collar folded into sharp triangles.
  - Chobby's deform bones hung from rigify helper bones that nothing moves: `reparent` in `Actor.BUNNY` re-hangs legs, arms, neck, chest and fingers under the spine and hands (no bone moves).
  - The player wants the chest soft and realistic, not pinned: "soft" parts in Gait follow the body through a damped spring (2.0 Hz, damping 0.27, lag at most 2.4% of height; made a little softer at the player's request). The clips swing the body about three times more than the old walk did, which kept the chest hanging at its limit ("limp", the player said), so only 60% of the spring's lag is shown (SOFT_GAIN) and at the limit the outward speed is lost. Steady walking only sways it a little; starts, steps and stops push it, and after a stop it swings on by inertia and settles. Keep it realistic, never exaggerated.
  - The step cadence was too fast for the player: strides are longer and grow with speed (bunny 1.45 m walking, 2.5 m running; TOFU 1.1 / 2.0).
  - Chobby's rest pose shows a V-sign with an arm at the face: Gait lowers the arms ("arms_down") and relaxes the fingers ("fingers": all three joints, the tip toward the real "_end" fingertip bone; otherwise the V-sign fingers stay straight).

## Taste (the player's preferences)
- Quiet, soft, non-ringing sounds; nothing shrill. Few notifications and toasts: show, don't tell.
- Real models and sounds, not placeholders (free CC0 packs: Kenney, KayKit, Quaternius; credits in CREDITS.md).
- Juice: things react when touched (a bounce, a puff, a sound), the camera shows what just opened.
- Phone first: the frame must stay smooth on a budget phone (Adreno 610). Measure, do not guess.
- Original game: the genre and mechanics may follow other cozy games, but no names, characters, art, music or maps copied from them.

## Running Godot here
- Godot 4.8 beta 1 (standard, not .NET). Headless tests: `godot --headless --path . --script tests/<suite>.gd`.
- Blender 5.2 lives in `/opt/blender` (install it again in a fresh container; the player allowed it). Scripts in `tools/blender/`.
- Crawl clips: `godot --headless --path . --script tools/make_crawl.gd` (rewrites `assets/anim/crawl.res`).
- Bunny model: `godot --headless --path . --script tools/tpose_bunny.gd`, then `godot --headless --path . --import` (rewrites `assets/chobby_tpose.glb`).
- Model checks: `tools/inspect_models.gd` (bones, clips, triangles), `tools/lineup.gd` (all models side by side), `tools/walk_clip.gd` (side clip: walk, stop, run, stop; add `drag` as the 5th argument for a long dragged run and stop; with `--write-movie`), `tools/shot.gd` (scripted play, screenshots; add `--write-movie x.png --fixed-fps 30` for a video).
- Pictures need a screen: `xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1920x1080x24" godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --script <script>` (headless draws nothing).
- Tests are small and check one thing each; every feature gets a test.

## Traps (from the previous project)
- No global shader uniforms (on the phone everything lit by them came out black); pass values as material uniforms.
- No `discard` and as little overdraw as possible in shaders: the phone pays per pixel.
- A freed object compares `== null` but is not nil: guard with `is_instance_valid`.
- Set a node's position before `add_child` when its `_ready` reads it.
- The default font lacks ✓, arrows and stars: draw icons with `_draw()`.
- A window that pauses the game must release the touch joystick when it closes.
